# LOOP demo video script

**Target duration:** 3–5 minutes  
**Use local URL:** `http://localhost:3100/login`  
**Demo login:** `demo@loop.app` / `LoopDemo!2026`

## 0:00–0:20 — Introduction

Show the login page.

> “Hi, this is LOOP, an AI customer-feedback intelligence platform. LOOP helps product teams turn scattered feedback into themes, trends, evidence-backed answers, and reports.”

Sign in with the demo account.

## 0:20–0:55 — Dashboard

Show the Dashboard after login. Point to the stat cards, chart, sentiment panel, and latest feedback.

> “The dashboard gives a quick picture of customer feedback: total volume, negative sentiment, new items, and themes that are growing. The charts make it easy to spot important changes without reading every response manually.”

Click the Hari Kumar account at the bottom of the sidebar and briefly show **Account settings** and **Log out**. Close it.

## 0:55–1:35 — Feedback inbox and ingestion

Open **Inbox**. Search for `onboarding`, then clear the search. Open **Filters** and choose a channel. Clear filters.

Click **Add feedback**, enter a short comment such as:

`Customers find it difficult to invite teammates during onboarding.`

Choose Support and click **Add feedback**.

Then click **Import CSV** and explain the accepted CSV columns.

> “Analysts can search, filter, triage, and add feedback individually. They can also import customer feedback in bulk from a CSV. In the production path, every incoming item is validated, tenant-scoped, and classified.”

## 1:35–2:00 — Trends

Open **Trends** and point to the momentum chart and rising signals.

> “The Trends view groups recurring feedback into themes and highlights what is growing compared with the previous period. This helps the team react to emerging issues early.”

## 2:00–2:35 — Ask LOOP

Open **Ask LOOP**. Use the suggested question:

`What are users saying about onboarding?`

Show the answer and its evidence card.

> “Ask LOOP turns feedback into a plain-English, evidence-backed answer. The response is grounded in the related customer feedback, so the team can see the evidence behind a recommendation.”

Optionally ask about mobile feedback or dashboard performance to show another answer.

## 2:35–3:10 — Voice-of-customer reports

Open **Reports**. Click **Generate report**, then **Open report**. Show the executive summary, evidence, and action recommendation. Click **Export**.

> “LOOP also creates a leadership-ready voice-of-customer report. It summarizes key themes, sentiment shifts, customer evidence, and recommended next actions. Reports can be opened and exported for sharing.”

## 3:10–3:35 — Workspace roles and support

Open **Settings**. Click **Invite member**, demonstrate the email and role selection, then close it. Click **View plan details**.

> “The application is designed for Admin, Analyst, and Viewer roles. Workspace access is enforced on the server, and all data is isolated by workspace.”

Open **Help** and click any guide.

## 3:35–3:55 — Closing

Return to the Dashboard.

> “LOOP closes the loop on customer feedback by helping teams capture signals, identify trends, ask better questions, and act on customer evidence. Thank you.”

## Recording checklist

- Use a browser window at 1280×720 or 1920×1080.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%.
- Turn off notifications before recording.
- Move the cursor slowly and pause for one second after each navigation.
- Record with Windows Snipping Tool, Xbox Game Bar (`Win + Alt + R`), or OBS Studio.
- Do not show `.env` files, terminal secrets, or personal accounts in the recording.
